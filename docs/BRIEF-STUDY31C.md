# Study 31c — amendment: the voted grid's whole-bar placement

Registered 2026-11-08, after Study 31b's committed run, before this
re-run of the A4 re-draft arm only. Study 31b's vote and witness
results stand (S31B-G1 PASS, A2 4/4); this amendment touches nothing
that feeds them.

**The defect.** A beat class names a lattice position modulo one bar,
so two anchors a whole bar apart share a class. The 31/31b runner
placed the voted anchor at base + shift beats, which on letter and
peaceful is the class's representative near bar TWO of the song; the
re-draft then index-paired grid bar 1 with chart bar 1 one bar out of
step, and the drafts collapsed below the deliberately wrong base
(68.0% and 61.4% vs 94.7% and 98.7% references). Study 30's peaceful
"61.4%" is the same defect: its novelty choice was the correct CLASS
and the committed study30 numbers are re-read accordingly (right class,
wrong whole-bar representative, and outside the absolute 0.1-beat
tolerance that study's gate used).

**The fix, registered before the re-run.** The voted anchor is the
class's LAST lattice point at or before the first audible sound plus a
quarter beat (bar one contains the first sound), fenced at never more
than one bar before the first sound. Predicted placements from
committed numbers: letter ≈ −0.079 s (truth −0.121), peaceful ≈
−0.057 s (truth −0.119), eat ≈ −0.070 s (truth −0.085, Correction 4),
mop ≈ −0.068 s (truth −0.066).

**The arm.** letter and peaceful re-drafted exactly as Studies 28/31
on the corrected placement. Gate S31C-G3 = S31-G3 unchanged: within 5
points of 94.7% and 98.7%. Spend ≈ $0.13; the 31-family total stays
inside the registered $0.5 fence (≈$0.40 after this run).

**Prediction.** Both drafts reach their references within 5 points —
the first chartless drafts ever to do so on these songs.
